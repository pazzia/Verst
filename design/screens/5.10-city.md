# 5.10 «По городу» — walk setup (Sprint 3, DES-302)

Mockup: `docs/design-canvas/Walk.dc.html` (presets · custom sheet · 18+ dialog · building · `NO_CITY` · `NOT_ENOUGH_POIS`) · Behaviour: CEO (3.2), Manager D6, **ADR-0013** (`city_presets`, `city_route`) · Components: §13, §21, §23 · Copy: copy-ru §15.3

## 1. Metaphor — stepping out of the car

A road trip is seen **through the windscreen**. A walk starts when you **step onto the pavement**:

- no roof liner, no A-pillars, no curved dashboard — the scene ends in a straight kerb;
- the scene is an evening street: facades, lit windows, street lamps, pavement;
- the overhead gantry sign becomes the Russian **street-name plate** (blue, white border, house-number square);
- the presets are **brown tourist plates**, the same brown 6.x language that marks sights on the road.

Body, chips, caption and the start button are the same system as the car screens, so both modes read as one product.

## 2. Entry

5.1 Windshield caption column: a plate `city_entry` «По городу» + `city_entry_sub` «пешком» with the `walker` icon, in the «Мои поездки» plate style (h 36, outline). Placed after «Мои поездки» on the same row (gap 8); alone when there are no saved trips. Back → Windshield.

The start = the current GPS origin (5.12) or the last chosen point; «изменить» opens the ОТКУДА search (towns, places, addresses).

## 5. Custom sheet («Свой маршрут», `BottomSheet`)

Title `city_custom_title`; rows (label `type.label.md` `color.text.muted` + `ParamChips`, gap 6, 16 between rows):

- СКОЛЬКО ГУЛЯЕМ: 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 ч (single);
- ЧТО ИНТЕРЕСНО: история, архитектура, музеи, парки, еда, бары, виды (multi, ≥ 1; «бары» triggers the 18+ dialog);
- ТЕМП: неспешно / обычный / бодро;
- БЮДЖЕТ НА ВСЕХ: до 2 000 / 5 000 / 10 000 ₽ / не важно;
- «Вернуться к старту» switch (default on).

Then `YellowButton` `city_build` «Собрать прогулку» (full width, h 56) — closes the sheet and builds. The caption title becomes «Свой маршрут · 4 ч».

## 6. 18+ dialog (every time)

`AgeGateDialog` (§23) **each time** an alcohol preset or the «бары» interest is chosen (not remembered, DEV-306). «Да, мне есть 18» → `adultConfirmed = true`; «Нет» → the selection is undone and **no request** is sent.

## 7. Building, empty states

- ПОШЛИ / «Собрать прогулку» → `city_route` (≤ 1.5 s): the caption block becomes a shimmer with `city_building` «Собираем прогулку…»; the start button shows the loading arc (components §7); the scene lamps keep glowing. Then → 5.11 (`motion.screen.arrive`). Cancel = back.
- `NO_CITY`: the preset grid is replaced by a plate (border `stroke.w1` `color.dash.outline`, radius 10, padding 12): `city_no_city_title` `type.title.sm`, `city_no_city_body` `type.body.md` `color.text.secondary`, `SecondaryButton` `city_no_city_action` «Выбрать старт». ПОШЛИ disabled.
- `NOT_ENOUGH_POIS`: the same plate with `city_not_enough_title` / `_body` and two buttons: «Гулять 6 ч» (next duration) and «Другой маршрут».
- Outside Russia: the 5.12 state.

## 8. TalkBack

Back → street plate (heading «По городу, старт: Советская площадь, 1, Ярославль») → start + «изменить» → presets (RadioButtons `cd_city_preset` / `cd_city_preset_18`) → caption (live) → «Пошли».

## 10. Sprint 4 — a fresh walk setup (CEO 3, 3.1, 3.2, 3.2.1; ADR-0015 §6; mockup `S4-CityStart.dc.html`)

The screen starts **from scratch**: it does not take the car crew, mood or budget. Order (scrolls; the caption row and ПОШЛИ stay pinned):

1. City scene + street plate (unchanged).
2. `city_start_label` «СТАРТ ПРОГУЛКИ» + **`StartField`** (components §27): an input-looking control, min-h 60, fill `#16222C`, 2 dp yellow border, locate icon 22, address 17/700 «Тверская ул., 7», sub `city_start_here` «Москва · вы здесь», and an «изменить» button with a pencil (h 40, fill `dash.control`). The whole field is one target → 5.12 search with focus on the field. It replaces the hint line «Старт: … изменить».
3. `city_crew_label` «КТО ИДЁТ»: three **`CrewCell`s** (§27) in a row — Взрослые (1–9, default 2), Дети (0–9, default 0), Собака (toggle, default off; paw icon; «с нами» / «нет»). Values are remembered for walks only.
4. `city_type_label` «КАКАЯ ПРОГУЛКА»: preset plates as before. With the dog on, **«Прогулка с собакой»** (`dog_walk`) is first; without the dog it is hidden. «Свой маршрут» is always last. Children **never** disable a preset (no «не с детьми», no toast).
5. Warnings directly under the presets (`WalkWarningPlate`, §28): child + drink preset → `walk_warn_child_bars`; dog + a preset with indoor stops → `walk_warn_dog_indoor` / `walk_warn_dog_bars` with actions «Прогулка с собакой» and «Собака останется дома». They are informative: ПОШЛИ stays enabled.
6. «СКОЛЬКО ГУЛЯЕМ»: 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 ч / **`city_duration_unlimited` «без ограничения»**, and a hint under the chips (`city_duration_hint_timed` / `_untimed`).
7. `city_prefs_label` «ПОЖЕЛАНИЯ И ОГРАНИЧЕНИЯ»: a settings plate with «Бюджет на всех ›», «Темп ›», «Вернуться к старту» (switch). Interests stay in «Свой маршрут».
8. Caption: «Пивной · без ограничения» / meta «2 взрослых · 1 ребёнок · собака · без расписания» (`city_caption_untimed`) or «… · старт 11:00».

**18+:** choosing a drink preset opens `AgeGateDialog` with **only** `age_gate_question` «Вам есть 18?» and «Нет» / «Да» (equal width, «Да» yellow). Asked every time; «Нет» keeps the previous preset.
