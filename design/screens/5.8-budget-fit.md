# 5.8 Budget fit — «Не помещается в бюджет» and the fit marks (Sprint 3, DES-300)

Mockup: `docs/design-canvas/Budget.dc.html` (states: no-fit · fitted Waybill lines · small overrun) · Behaviour: CEO (1), Manager D1, **ADR-0011** · Components: §6, §15, §20 · Copy: copy-ru §15.1

## 1. The rule (engine-side, ADR-0011; the app only displays it)

`allowed = budget + min(round(0.05 · budget), 3000)`. The engine runs the lever ladder (cheaper hotels + family rooms → cheaper food → free sights → fewer nights → short variant) and returns either a plan (`outcome = plan`, maybe with `budgetFit`) or `outcome = no_fit`.

| Result | `budgetFit.status` | UI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| first plan ≤ budget | (absent) | as today; Road footer badge `budget_fit_badge` «В БЮДЖЕТЕ» |
| fitted ≤ budget | `fits` | badge «В БЮДЖЕТЕ»; Waybill lever lines (§3) |
| fitted ≤ allowed | `small_overrun` | badge `budget_tight_badge` «ЧУТЬ БОЛЬШЕ: +1 200 ₽»; Waybill lever lines; «Остаток» → «Сверх бюджета» in ink |
| even the minimum > allowed | — (`no_fit`) | **this screen** (§2) instead of Road ahead |
| budget «не важно» | — | no budget marks at all |

## 2. The no-fit screen (replaces Road ahead after Drive)

Metaphor: the prohibitory round sign **«Ограничение массы»** (GOST 3.11) — red ring, white field — carrying the budget instead of tonnes: the trip «не проходит» under it. Same strip, cap and body as Road ahead, so the user feels they arrived at the same place and hit a sign.

At 390 × 844 with 3 options everything fits (N7 bottom ≈ 700). Shorter windows scroll the body (below the strip) under a 16 dp fade.

Behaviour (DEV-303): an option opens **Instruments** with the option applied (budget set to `budgetRub` — the gauge shows the exact value «до 42 000 ₽», the needle at the interpolated position between presets; or the ready route / `days` changed) and the user presses ПОЕХАЛИ again → Drive → a plan that fits. «Показать самый экономный вариант» opens Road ahead with the `minimum.trip`, marked over budget everywhere: footer `road_waybill_over` in `color.paper.inkRed`, Waybill «Перерасход» + `budget_refit` «Подогнать под бюджет».

Entry: Drive's success CTA is replaced by `budget_nofit_cta` «Смотреть варианты» (`PrimaryCta`, arrow) — auto-advance to this screen after 1 s as for a plan. Stage line during fitting: engine «Подбираем под бюджет: дешевле ночёвки» (key `budget`).

TalkBack: on open, polite `cd_budget_nofit`; the sign is decorative; options are Buttons `cd_budget_option`.
