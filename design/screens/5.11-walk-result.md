## 9. S3-QA additions

- **Start time (P0-01).** The header and the 5.10 caption show the same time: the engine's `startAt` in the town's time zone (Architect: `tz` per town in `city_presets`). A walk never starts at night: day presets and custom walks start between 09:00 and 20:00 − duration (so they end by 22:00); drink presets between 18:00 and 23:00 − duration. If «now, rounded up to 15 min» is later than the window, the walk starts at the window's opening on the next day and the copy says so: caption `city_caption_meta_tomorrow` «Ярославль · старт завтра в 10:00», header `walk_header_tomorrow` «Завтра: старт 10:00 · финиш ≈ 13:52». Before the window opens today → the opening time today.
- **Shorter than asked (P1-02).** When the engine's walk is shorter than the preset / chosen duration by ≥ 30 min, the duration chip shows the real duration and a note `walk_shorter` «Получилось 2 ч вместо 4 ч — больше открытых мест рядом нет» (`type.body.sm` `color.text.muted`) sits under the ±15 % line. The 5.10 plate sub keeps the preset's nominal duration.
- **Free walk (P1-03).** Total 0 ₽ → footer amount `walk_footer_free` «Бесплатно», right line `walk_footer_free_sub` «вход везде свободный»; no per-person line; never «≈ 0 ₽».
- **Km format (P2).** Title sign and chip use the deck's km rule: one decimal below 10 km **without a trailing zero** («≈ 6 км», «≈ 5,8 км»).
- **Distances under 1 km**: `walk_distance_m` «600 м» (rounded to 50 m); from 1 km `common_km` «1,2 км».
