## 3. Hotel card — mandatory area labelling

Synthetic and fixture hotels are fictional (ADR-0012 §4). Their card shows the **town / area photo** (`image.isArea = true`) with:

- the plate `card_area_plate` «ФОТО РАЙОНА» on the photo (top-left, §18);
- the credit line `card_photo_area` «Фото района: Ярославль · Wikimedia Commons» (engine `labelRu`);
- `card_hotel_fictional` under the frame.

A hotel card never shows a photo without all three; with no area photo it shows the `city` placeholder and keeps the note. Facts: tags «можно с собакой · семейный номер · парковка»; price «≈ 6 000 ₽ за ночь» or «забронировано».

**When the area photo is a placeholder** (none / error / offline, S3-QA P1-05): hide the `card_photo_area` credit (a credit names a photo that is on screen) and show `card_hotel_fictional_nophoto` «Гостиница в демо-сборке вымышленная» instead of `card_hotel_fictional`. The fictional status is said once: no «Демо: вымышленная гостиница» line among the facts.

## 4. Photo states (§18)

loading (placeholder illustration + shimmer) → loaded crop (landscape centre, portrait upper third) | loaded contain on the mat (tiny, extreme aspect, transparent) | placeholder with `card_photo_none` (no file / no free licence) | `card_photo_error` (HTTP error, 10 s timeout) | `card_photo_offline` (offline and not in the 64 MB cache). The frame never changes size.

## 5. Return to the row (CEO 2.1, M§15.4, DEV-305)

`focusStopId` is set when a card, the Exit/booking flow or a hand-off opens from a row. On return the list puts that row back at the **same viewport offset** (`bringIntoView`, no animation), TalkBack focus moves to it, and the row plays `motion.returnToItem`: background `color.overlay.returnFlash` (accent yellow 14 %) for 400 ms, then fades out over 800 ms; the Waybill footer (not a row) restores position only. Survives process death (`last.json`). If the stop was replaced meanwhile, the replacement row is the target.

## 6. TalkBack

Pane `cd_card`; order: name (heading) → kind line → photo (`cd_card_photo` / `cd_card_photo_area`) → credit (`cd_card_credit`, link) → description → facts → actions → report → OSM line.
